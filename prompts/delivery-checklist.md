# Delivery Checklist — 9:16 Hero/Heroine Cut

Run top to bottom. Any unchecked box is a delivery blocker. Test on a calibrated display
**and** on a 6-inch phone at arm's length, once with sound and once muted.

## 1. Format & Technical

- [ ] Timeline is a true 9:16 (1080 × 1920 / 2160 × 3840 / 4320 × 7680) — no bars, no matte
- [ ] No stretching, squeezing, or non-uniform scaling anywhere
- [ ] Frame rate matches source, no dropped or duplicated frames on cuts
- [ ] Color space tagged correctly (Rec.709 / Rec.2020 PQ) and consistent across masters
- [ ] 8K vertical master exported (ProRes 4444 XQ / DNxHR 444 level)
- [ ] 4K vertical master exported (HEVC 10-bit, 120–180 Mbps)
- [ ] 1080 × 1920 platform copies exported (H.264 High, 20–45 Mbps, 2-pass)
- [ ] Clean version (no burned-in text) exported
- [ ] `.srt` and styled `.ass` lyric files exported
- [ ] Audio at −14 LUFS integrated, true peak −1.0 dBTP, no clipping, sync verified
- [ ] Grader stills exported (before/after) for the emotional beats

## 2. Reframe — Hero & Heroine

- [ ] Eyes in the upper third, inside the prime zone, in **every** shot with the hero or heroine
- [ ] No shot cuts through a face, chin, mouth, ear, or forehead
- [ ] Body crops land between joints (mid-thigh, above knee, mid-forearm)
- [ ] 4–7% headroom, 3–5% footroom on full-body shots
- [ ] Look room preserved; eyeline direction matches the original in every shot
- [ ] True two-shots resolved by alternating coverage; listeners' reactions included
- [ ] Every emotional peak framed on eyes and mouth, held long enough to land
- [ ] Wide/establishing shots resolved by reveal or split-stack, never a letterboxed strip
- [ ] Tracked reframes free of jitter, warp, edge crawl, and rubber-banding (frame-by-frame)
- [ ] Reframe motion motivated and eased (6–12 frames), landed on beats where it cuts
- [ ] No face or key action inside the top 12% or bottom 25% platform dead zones
- [ ] Nothing upscaled beyond native resolution, or the upscale is documented and approved

## 3. Color

- [ ] Corrected on the native 8K source in 32-bit float, scene-referred, before the creative grade
- [ ] White balance neutralized on a known reference in every shot
- [ ] Contrast pivoted on skin luminance; faces do not shift as contrast moves
- [ ] Highlight rolloff is smooth — no clipped windows, candles, or speculars
- [ ] Black point set; dark skin, dark hair, and shadow detail retain texture
- [ ] Skin vector sits on the 11 o'clock line; skin luminance plausible for each subject
- [ ] Secondaries tracked, feathered, and invisible; hero/heroine are the brightest and sharpest elements
- [ ] Backgrounds separated with subtle desaturation/cooling only where the cut needs it
- [ ] One documented cinematic palette, applied consistently
- [ ] Grain sized for 8K and scaled to delivery; halation and bloom subtle
- [ ] No banding in skies or gradients; no chroma noise; no sharpening halos
- [ ] Shot-matched against the stills gallery — skin, luminance, saturation, contrast
- [ ] Continuity holds across day/night, interiors/exteriors, cameras, and scenes shot apart

## 4. Kinetic Typography

- [ ] One locked type system (display + support + optional accent), presets saved and reused
- [ ] Fonts are licensed; any substitute is documented with its visible differences
- [ ] Reference match completed: font, weight, size ratio, tracking, hex fills, gradient,
      stroke, shadow, glow, dimension, IN/OUT motion signature, emphasis device
- [ ] Every text IN/OUT snapped to the beat grid; text arrives with the vocal attack
- [ ] Zero frames of drift between lyric and vocal (verified on the waveform)
- [ ] Minimum hold 0.8 s respected; fast lines split rather than shrunk
- [ ] Keywords emphasized on downbeats; hook treatment identical every repeat
- [ ] Custom easing on every keyframe; motion blur at 180° shutter
- [ ] 1–2 signature moves per section; no style changes line to line
- [ ] Motion tracking / keyframing locked to plate; occlusion (if used) passes behind hair,
      never across features
- [ ] Depth treatments (shadow, glow, stroke, extrusion, light wrap) sit the type *in* the scene
- [ ] Max 3 lines, max 20–24 characters per line, cap height ≥ ~70 px at 1080 × 1920
- [ ] Contrast 4.5:1 or better; scrim/dim used instead of hard boxes on busy plates
- [ ] No text over the hero's or heroine's eyes or mouth at any emotional beat
- [ ] Text and subject occupy different vertical bands, balanced in the 9:16 frame
- [ ] Test renders approved on a phone screen before full-timeline animation
- [ ] Title/artist end card in the same system, inside safe zones, held to the last music frame

## 5. Final Watch

- [ ] Full playthrough with headphones, watching **only** the text timing
- [ ] Full playthrough watching **only** the hero and heroine — text never fights them
- [ ] Full playthrough muted — the film still communicates emotionally without sound
- [ ] First 3 seconds hook the viewer with a hero/heroine face, legible and in focus
- [ ] Last frame is intentional (held, composed, no accidental freeze or black)
- [ ] Change log written: every reframe decision, grade intent, documented deviation, and
      anything not achieved at the target quality bar

QUICK-PASTE VERSION - 9:16 Vertical Reframe + 8K Color + Kinetic Typography
(Hero & Heroine Face/Body Priority. Use when the input field is short. The full brief is in
vertical-9x16-hero-heroine-master-prompt.md.)

---

You are a senior colorist and motion-graphics designer finishing a vertical music video.

GOAL: Convert the supplied footage from its original aspect ratio to 9:16, graded in 8K and
finished with kinetic lyric typography, keeping the HERO AND HEROINE's faces and bodies as the
constant visual center of gravity.

REFRAME (9:16):
- Work from the original 8K master. A native 9:16 window in an 8K 16:9 frame is only
  2430x4320, so treat this as a re-composition, not a crop. Never letterbox, never stretch,
  never auto-crop unsupervised.
- Anchor on the EYES: place them on the upper third, within the middle band of the frame.
  Never cut through a face, chin, mouth, ear, or forehead. Land body cuts between joints
  (mid-thigh, above knee, mid-forearm) - never at wrist, elbow, knee, or ankle.
- Keep 4-7% headroom, 3-5% footroom on full-body shots, and 1/3 look room in the direction
  of the subject's gaze. Preserve eyeline matches; never mirror a shot to force composition.
- Two-shots: alternate hero coverage and heroine coverage, cutting to the LISTENER's reaction
  on the last word of each line. Every emotional peak is framed tight enough to show eyes and
  mouth and held long enough to land.
- Wide shots: slow vertical reveal or matched two-band split-stack - never a letterboxed strip.
- Reframe per shot with eased keyframes (6-12 frame eases), tracked and hand-corrected for
  drift, no jitter or rubber-banding. Supersample: keyframe in 8K, deliver downscaled.
- Keep faces and all key action out of the top 12% and bottom 25% of the frame (platform UI).

COLOR (8K):
- Correct on the native 8K/RAW source in 32-bit float on a scene-referred timeline
  (ACEScct or DaVinci Wide Gamut) before any creative grade.
- Primary: exposure with retained highlight headroom, black point set, contrast pivoted on
  skin luminance, white balance neutralized on a known reference, smooth highlight rolloff,
  no clipping, no crushed shadows - dark skin and dark hair must keep texture.
- Secondary: soft tracked windows on each face for a subtle midtone lift, gentle warmth
  inside the skin-tone vector (11 o'clock on the vectorscope), shine control, subtle eye
  contrast with catchlight glow, light lip saturation at the emotional peaks. Keep masks
  invisible; the hero and heroine stay the brightest, sharpest elements in frame.
- Creative grade: one cinematic palette for the film (warm amber romance, skin-protected
  teal & orange, desaturated moody, Kodak 2383 emulation, pastel dream, sun-baked desert, or
  night neon), applied with film emulation, halation, finely scaled grain, and a 5-12% vignette.
  Use LUTs as a starting point, never as the finish.
- Consistency: build a stills gallery of hero frames and split-screen match every shot -
  skin first, then luminance, saturation, contrast - across day/night, interiors/exteriors,
  and different cameras.
- Preserve 8K clarity: no added noise, no banding, no sharpening halos, no 8-bit round trips.
  Export an 8K vertical master (4320x7680 ProRes 4444 XQ or equivalent) plus 4K and
  1080x1920 platform versions, and before/after stills.

KINETIC TYPOGRAPHY:
- Build one type system: a display face for the song's emotional voice, a support face, and
  an optional accent face. Match the mood (bold/anthemic, modern pop, elegant romantic,
  urgent condensed). Lock weight, tracking, leading, case, and alignment; save every animation
  as a reusable preset.
- Sync precisely: derive the BPM grid, place beat markers, and snap every text IN/OUT to a
  beat. Text arrives 2-4 frames before the vocal attack or exactly on the downbeat. Exit on
  the breath or the beat. Minimum hold 0.8 s. Per-syllable reveals for fast delivery,
  per-line for legato. Check sync frame by frame on the waveform; 1 frame of drift is visible.
- Animate with fades, slides, scale pops (92% -> 100% with slight overshoot), mask wipes,
  per-character stagger, typewriter, karaoke fill, and emphasis moves on keywords. Custom
  easing on every keyframe; 180-degree motion blur; 1-2 signature moves per section, never a
  new style per line. No spinning type, no unmotivated fly-ins.
- Add depth: soft drop shadows, glow, stroke for contrast, subtle 3D extrusion, texture,
  light wrap. Motion-track or keyframe the text to the plate. Where it serves the emotion,
  roto the hero or heroine so a lyric passes behind their head - never across their features.
- Legibility: max 3 lines, max 20-24 characters per line, minimum ~70 px cap height at
  1080x1920, 4.5:1 contrast or better, soft gradient scrim or 10-25% dim behind type on busy
  plates rather than a hard box. Never place text in the top 12% or bottom 25% dead zones,
  and never over the hero's or heroine's eyes or mouth. Text and subject occupy different
  vertical bands.

REFERENCE MATCH:
- Analyze the reference video and extract, explicitly: font (identify it; if unavailable or
  unlicensed, choose the closest licensed match and say so), weight, size as a percentage of
  frame height, tracking, leading, case, fill hex and gradient stops, stroke, shadow, glow,
  dimensional treatment, effect layers, IN and OUT animation with durations and easing,
  emphasis device, layout habits, and the full color palette. Reproduce the load-bearing
  attributes and the motion signature exactly, adapting only for legibility against this film
  and harmony with its grade. Document every deviation. Apply the locked style to every text
  element - lyrics, ad-libs, hook repeats, title and end cards - then side-by-side test against
  the reference in motion at full and quarter speed until the seam disappears.

LYRICS:
- Lay the supplied lyrics on the vocal line by line and word by word. Give each song section
  a distinct but related treatment (verses restrained, choruses with the signature move and
  full-frame emphasis words, bridge minimal). Emphasize key words with color shifts, scaling,
  or highlighting. Repeat the hook treatment identically every time. End on a title/artist
  card in the same system. Deliver burned-in text plus styled .srt/.ass.

DELIVERY: 8K vertical master, 4K vertical master, 1080x1920 platform cuts, a clean version
with no text, styled .srt/.ass, graded before/after stills, and a change log listing every
reframe decision, every grade intent, every documented deviation from the reference, and
anything you could not achieve at this quality bar. QC on a calibrated display and on a
6-inch phone before delivery.
